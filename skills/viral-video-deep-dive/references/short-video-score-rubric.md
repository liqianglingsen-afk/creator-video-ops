# Short Video Score Rubric

Keep this historical rubric only when revisiting an existing report that used it. For new teardowns, follow the [current ten-dimension rubric](../../short-video-forensic-teardown/references/score-rubric.md) and [existing-footage edit plan](../../short-video-forensic-teardown/references/edit-plan.md), with a minimum optimization target of 85/100. Every score must be backed by concrete evidence: timestamp, frame/screenshot, subtitle line, transcript line, page caption, media probe output, or file metadata.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | What to inspect |
|---|---:|---|
| 首屏钩子 | 10 | First 0-3 seconds: user benefit, conflict, curiosity, result promise, strong visual, scroll-stop power. |
| 选题价值 | 10 | Audience pain point, novelty, usefulness, emotional relevance, market/category fit. |
| 脚本结构 | 10 | Hook, setup, tension, evidence/story blocks, turning points, payoff, CTA. |
| 拍摄构图 | 10 | Framing, subject placement, foreground/background depth, lighting, color, location, props. |
| 运镜覆盖 | 8 | Push/pull/pan/tilt/follow/static balance, shot variety, movement motivation, stability. |
| 剪辑节奏 | 8 | Cut timing, beat matching, shot duration, pattern interrupts, pacing variation. |
| 高光同步 | 10 | Highlight lines synced across audio emphasis, visual emphasis, subtitle emphasis, and edit emphasis. |
| 字幕设计 | 8 | Font, size, position, safe area, line breaks, keyword hierarchy, stroke/shadow/background. |
| 包装层级 | 7 | Title cards, identity tags, question cards, masks, overlays, alignment, brand consistency. |
| 声音质量 | 5 | Speech intelligibility, music balance, noise, loudness, emotional sound design. |
| CTA 转化 | 6 | Clear ask, value exchange, timing, objection handling, follow/lead/sales intent. |
| 技术质量 | 4 | Verified source resolution, bitrate/codec, compression, frame quality, extraction confidence. |

## Output Rules

- Include current score and target score after optimization.
- Do not use vague comments such as "还不错" without evidence.
- If a dimension cannot be verified, mark it `未验证` and explain what evidence is missing; do not award full points by assumption.
- For weak dimensions, provide the optimized replacement plan rather than a replication plan unless the user explicitly requested full replication.

## Suggested Score Bands

- 85-100: Can be used as a benchmark template.
- 70-84: Strong video with clear optimizable weak points.
- 55-69: Usable material, weak viral architecture.
- 40-54: Mainly documentary/recording value; needs restructuring.
- Below 40: Major script/visual/technical problems.
